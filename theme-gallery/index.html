Doom Emacs Theme Gallery

How to use

Quick guide

Select

Click a theme tile on the left. The right panel updates.

Image

  • Click the preview to open Fullscreen.
  • Click left/right halves or press [/].

Compare

  • Click Compare.
  • Your current theme is A. Click another tile for B.
  • Drag the ↔ handle to reveal each side. Use ←/→ or </> for fine control. Press and hold Space to quick-flip A/B.

Live

Shows the selected theme in the live preview pane.

Keys

[/] prev/next · F fullscreen · I image · L live · C compare · A apply · Esc close
