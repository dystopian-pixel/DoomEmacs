This section is a hub for everyday programming workflow comforts. It gathers small, reliable aids that speed up common tasks and keep momentum steady. ** C++ Compilation

This subsection centers on turning your C++ work into a runnable result with minimal ceremony. It emphasizes quick loops so you can make a change, check the outcome, and keep moving. The goal is steady rhythm over complexity—fast runs, clear outcomes, and fewer interruptions. It helps your experiments feel light, safe, and repeatable.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
;;; language-development.el --- C++ helpers -*- lexical-binding: t; -*-

(defun my/compile-cpp-project ()
  "Compile all C++ files."
  (interactive)
  (compile "g++ -std=c++17 -O2 $(find . -name '*.cpp') -o app"))
#+end_src
