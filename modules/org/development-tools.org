* Development Tools Configuration

This section is the hub where your day-to-day coding helpers are shaped into a calm, reliable toolkit. It focuses on predictable behavior and smooth feedback so you can keep momentum without wrestling settings. The aim is to make your editor feel like a steady collaborator across different projects. You get consistency, fewer surprises, and a workspace that anticipates common routines.

** LSP Mode

This subsection adds a layer of “smart awareness” to your editing sessions. It helps the editor understand the shape of your work so navigation, hints, and quick insights feel natural. The goal is gentle guidance that keeps you oriented without getting in the way. You spend less time hunting and more time shaping ideas.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(after! lsp-mode
  (setq lsp-enable-symbol-highlighting t
        lsp-enable-on-type-formatting nil))
#+end_src

** Company Mode

Here the editing flow is supported with helpful suggestions that appear right when you need them. It encourages a steady rhythm by reducing guesswork and extra keystrokes. The emphasis is on clarity and discoverability so choices are easy to scan and select. You stay in the zone while the editor quietly lends a hand.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(after! company
  (setq company-idle-delay 0.2
        company-minimum-prefix-length 1))
#+end_src
